Flight Sim – Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 26, 2026
Developer: Christina Thaler, United States of America
Contact: JohnSmithMarston@outlook.com

Flight Sim (“the app”) is a flight simulator for macOS. This policy explains what information the app uses and where it goes. In short: we do not collect your personal information. There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

1. Information we do not collect

  • We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or any account.
  • We do not use advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, microphone, camera or the location of your Mac.
  • We do not sell or share personal data, because we do not hold any.

2. Information stored on your Mac

Your game progress (gold, unlocked aircraft and airports, completed missions, settings and key bindings) and a cache of downloaded map tiles are stored only on your Mac, inside the app’s own storage area. They are never sent to us. Deleting the app removes them.

3. Network requests the app makes

The app needs an internet connection to show the real world. While you fly, it requests map and data files from the third-party services below. Each request contains the map area or flight position in the game that it asks about (not the location of your Mac) and, as with any internet request, your Mac’s IP address and the app name are visible to that service. We do not run these services and do not receive these requests.

Each service has its own privacy policy. You can switch live traffic and live weather off in the game’s Display menu; the game then makes fewer requests.

4. Speech and sound

Spoken radio calls and alerts use the speech synthesis built into macOS and run on your Mac. The app does not record audio.

5. Purchases

Aircraft, airports and carriers are bought with in-game gold earned by playing. The app has no real-money purchases, and we collect no payment information.

6. Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your country).

7. Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you contact us by email, we use your message only to reply to you, and delete it when the matter is closed. Under laws such as the GDPR and CCPA you may ask us what data we hold about you, and we will confirm that we hold none other than any email you sent us.

8. Security

The app runs in the macOS App Sandbox and connects to the services above only over secure (HTTPS) connections.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the new version at this address with a new effective date.
